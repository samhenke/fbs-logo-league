Pecking Order

How to play

  1. Choose a deck. Each game has three rounds of four cards.
  2. Move the cards into ascending order using the up and down buttons, or drag them into place.
  3. Lock in your order. Every pair you placed in the right order earns a point, for up to six per round.
  4. Send your challenge link to a friend. They get the same cards and starting order, with your score to beat.
