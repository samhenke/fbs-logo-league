How to play

An emoji can stand for a thing, its sound, or the letters in its name. Read the whole scene: an equation, a price, or a change from one picture to the next can complete the clue.

Type your guess and press Enter. Each solve earns one point. You can try again or pass and return to that puzzle later. The clock keeps running.

End this round?

Your current score will be final. The timer is still running.
